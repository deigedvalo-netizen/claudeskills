# ADR-011: Machine-read values are provisional until confirmed, enforced by a finalisation gate

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** wallcovering-takeoff
- **Requirements:** NFR-4, FR-7, FR-8

## Context

The source is explicit that no auto-read value is final until the estimator confirms it, and that the app assists rather than autonomously producing an order. A vision adapter can return a plausible but wrong dimension, which is undetectable downstream once it becomes a number.

## Decision

Proposals carry confirmed=False by default and only a ConfirmedValue may enter TakeoffEngine. ConfirmationGate exposes is_takeoff_finalisable, which reports every unconfirmed field and every blocking flag, and ExportService refuses to emit a final takeoff while either list is non-empty.

## Consequences

Positive: NFR-4 is enforced at a single checkable choke point rather than distributed across the UI, and the manual v1 path satisfies it trivially since entry is confirmation. Negative: the vision pre-fill delivers less time saving than full automation, which is the intended trade.
