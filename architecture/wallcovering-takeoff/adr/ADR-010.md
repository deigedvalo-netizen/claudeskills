# ADR-010: Takeoff policy is explicit configuration captured as a snapshot per result

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** wallcovering-takeoff
- **Requirements:** FR-4, FR-5, NFR-1, NFR-2

## Context

Opening-deduction practice, its size threshold, waste percentage and trim allowance all vary by estimator and by job. Hard-coding them makes results unexplainable and non-reproducible once a default changes.

## Decision

PolicyStore holds waste percentage with optional per-WC override, the opening-deduction toggle and its area threshold, and the trim allowance. Every QuantityResult records the PolicySnapshot it was computed under, and the presentation and export layers display that snapshot alongside the numbers.

## Consequences

Positive: NFR-2 holds across default changes because a result names its own settings, and the policy summary required by the output design is available directly. Negative: policy must be threaded into geometry and quantity calls rather than read from a module-level constant.
