# ADR-009: Wall identity is (room_id, wall_id) and drives cross-sheet de-duplication

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** wallcovering-takeoff
- **Requirements:** FR-6, FR-4, FR-8

## Context

The same physical wall commonly appears on both the finish plan and an elevation. Counting it from each sheet double-counts the material, while dropping one sheet loses the partial-height and opening information the elevation carries.

## Decision

Give every wall a stable identity of (room_id, wall_id) and merge all sheet observations onto it: width is taken from the finish plan, applied height from the elevation when it differs from ceiling height, and openings from whichever sheet documents them. Aggregation de-duplicates on this identity, and a width disagreement between plan and elevation raises CONFLICT rather than picking a winner.

## Consequences

Positive: AC-6.2 is satisfied structurally and elevation-only information is preserved. Negative: matching an elevation to the correct wall becomes a required resolution step, and unmatched elevations must be flagged rather than silently ignored.
