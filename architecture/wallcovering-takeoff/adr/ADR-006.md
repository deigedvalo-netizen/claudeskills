# ADR-006: Waste is applied exactly once, structurally separate from repeat loss

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** wallcovering-takeoff
- **Requirements:** FR-5, FR-6

## Context

Pattern-repeat loss and waste percentage cover different things, and folding repeat loss into the waste percentage inflates every order. The source calls this out explicitly and requires that setting waste to zero still leaves the repeat-driven quantity intact.

## Decision

The strip method computes repeat loss inside cut_length via ceil((height + trim)/repeat) * repeat and reports raw_qty with that loss already included and no waste. Waste is applied once afterwards, at a single call site, producing with_waste_qty. No function accepts an already-wasted quantity and applies waste again, and raw and with-waste values are carried side by side to the presentation layer.

## Consequences

Positive: AC-5.4 is verifiable by toggling waste to zero and observing the repeat-driven quantity survive, and double-counting is prevented by the shape of the data rather than by care. Negative: two quantity fields must be threaded through aggregation, export and display instead of one.
